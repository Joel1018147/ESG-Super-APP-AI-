## Provenance note — READ BEFORE THIS PAGE IS PROMOTED

Every file above **except** `hero.mp4` / `hero-poster.jpg` is from Pexels under a
licence permitting commercial use with no attribution.

The hero reel was supplied by the client. Its licence has **not** been
established, and there is now concrete reason to establish it: the last ten
seconds of the source carry a **YouTube end-card** — "Subscribe / Our Channel /
Previous Uploads / Next Videos" over the standard end-screen placeholder boxes.
That is what a YouTube download looks like, not what licensed stock footage
looks like.

The card is trimmed out of what ships (the cut is at 319.8s, immediately before
it), so no third-party branding or call-to-action appears on the landing page.
Trimming it removes the *symptom*. It does not resolve the question of who owns
the footage, and that question is now live: the video is publicly served from
the platform's front page.

What would settle it: the original source of the reel and whatever licence came
with it. If none can be produced, the Pexels library already used for every
other asset on this page has equivalent footage that is clear for commercial
use, and swapping it is a one-command change.
