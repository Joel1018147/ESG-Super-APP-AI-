What this platform does not claim

Stated up front, because a counterparty will ask and the answer should be the same on the website as it is inside the product.

Not a certification

A score here is a self-assessment supported by evidence. It is not an audit, not an assurance opinion, and not a certificate.

Aligned, not compliant

The assessment is aligned in draft to Malaysia's Simplified ESG Disclosure Guide. The official disclosure set is published in full inside the platform, and the reconciliation against it is not finished.

The model never writes a number

AI drafts recommendations and proposes answers from your documents. It is structurally prevented from producing or altering a score.

Governance

Owned by SSEO, versioned like a standard

The Malaysia SMEs ESG e-Reporting System is registered under the SMEs Sustainable Entrepreneur Organisation (SSEO) as the official platform for participating SME organisations and ESG reporting initiatives.

SSEO owns the assessment framework and is the body that revises it. A revision is issued as a new framework version, never as an edit to the one companies have already been scored against.

The platform covers self-assessment and evidence. Independent verification, certification, and investor or customer due diligence sit outside it and are performed by other parties.

Supporting partners

The types of organisation this platform is built to work alongside, and the role each would play. No organisation is named here, and none of this describes work already done — a partner appears on this page when there is an agreement to point at.

Universities & research institutions

The role Reviewing the methodology behind the indicator set and the emission factors, and testing the assessment against what an SME can realistically evidence.

ESG consultants

The role Working alongside a company through the disclosures, and turning a completed assessment into the changes that move a score rather than a report that sits still.

Professional bodies

The role Anchoring the framework to the standards their members already carry, and giving the assessment recognition inside the professions SMEs depend on.

Financial institutions

The role Reading a completed assessment as one input to sustainable finance and ESG-linked lending, with the evidence tier visible behind every answer.

Technology providers

The role Connecting the systems an SME already runs — utilities, accounting, operations — so activity data reaches the carbon module without being re-keyed.

Sustainability assurance partners

The role Performing the independent verification this platform deliberately does not, which is what moves an answer from documented to third-party verified.

Who we are

The Malaysia SMEs ESG e-Reporting System gives Malaysian SMEs one place to answer the ESG disclosures their customers, their banks and their supply chains are already asking for.

Everything it reports is traceable: an answer, the evidence tier behind it, and the framework and engine versions the score was produced under. Nothing is asserted that the record cannot support.
