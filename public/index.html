What it does

Four things, and it is honest about where each one stops.

Assess

A structured questionnaire across Environmental, Social and Governance. Quantitative questions score on disclosure, not on whether the number is good — inventing a pass mark would be a judgement nobody has agreed.

Evidence

Upload a policy, a utility bill or a full report. An answer backed by a document scores higher than one that is only declared, and the platform records which is which.

Carbon

Scope 1 and Scope 2 from activity data, using published emission factors. Every entry stores the factor, its version and its source, so a figure can be re-checked years later.

Read your own documents

The system reads an uploaded report and proposes answers, each with the quoted sentence it came from. Nothing is recorded until a person accepts it.

How a company uses it

1
Register

Company details, sector and location, so the right emission factors apply.

2
Answer

Work through the disclosures. Not applicable is a real answer and is treated as one.

3
Attach evidence

Upload what you already have. Review what the system proposes from it.

4
Score and share

A score with its framework version, weighting version and engine version attached.

What this platform does not claim

Stated up front, because a counterparty will ask and the answer should be the same on the website as it is inside the product.

Not a certification

A score here is a self-assessment supported by evidence. It is not an audit, not an assurance opinion, and not a certificate.

Aligned, not compliant

The assessment is aligned in draft to Malaysia's Simplified ESG Disclosure Guide. The official disclosure set is published in full inside the platform, and the reconciliation against it is not finished.

The model never writes a number

AI drafts recommendations and proposes answers from your documents. It is structurally prevented from producing or altering a score.

Governance

Platform owner. SMEs Sustainable Entrepreneur Organisation (SSEO). SSEO owns the assessment framework and is the body that revises it. A revision is issued as a new framework version, never as an edit to the one companies have already been scored against.

The platform covers self-assessment and evidence. Independent verification, certification, and investor or customer due diligence sit outside it and are performed by other parties.
